About

Y Combinator is a startup accelerator founded in 2005 that invests a fixed amount in early-stage companies and runs them through a three-month program. Each batch ends in Demo Day, where founders present to investors.

Batch Highlights

Programs

Core Program

Three months of group office hours, weekly dinners, and Demo Day.

Startup School

A free online course covering the basics of starting a company.

Alumni Network

Ongoing access to thousands of founders after the batch ends.
